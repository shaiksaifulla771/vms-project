# Visitor & Facility Management Skill

## Overview
Equips AI agents to manage physical visitor appointments, generate secure check-in QR codes, enforce host notifications, and maintain multi-site gate logs.

## Capabilities & Tools
1. `visitor_pre_registration`: Captures visitor details, purpose, company, and expected arrival time.
2. `host_approval_dispatcher`: Dispatches real-time approval requests to host employees via email/SMS.
3. `qr_badge_generator`: Generates temporary digital access pass and check-in QR code.
4. `gate_checkin_checkout`: Records entry/exit timestamps and assigns visitor badges.
5. `blacklist_watchdog`: Flags banned or high-risk visitors during pre-registration.
6. `nda_safety_briefing_tracker`: Ensures visitors acknowledge NDA and safety protocols before entry.
7. `multi_site_access_scope`: Restricts visitor passes to specific buildings and operational sites.
8. `overstay_alerting_engine`: Flags visitors remaining on premises after scheduled appointment end.
9. `emergency_evacuation_roster`: Produces live real-time headcount of all on-site visitors and contractors.
10. `visitor_audit_log_exporter`: Exports compliance-ready gate logs for security audits.
