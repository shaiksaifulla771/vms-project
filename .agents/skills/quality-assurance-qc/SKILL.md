# Quality Assurance & QC Inspection Skill

## Overview
Equips AI agents to evaluate material quality test records, enforce pass/fail standards, manage quarantined inventory, and trigger corrective action workflows.

## Capabilities & Tools
1. `aql_sample_size_calculator`: Determines statistical sampling sizes based on ANSI/ASQ standards.
2. `incoming_inspection_check`: Validates incoming raw materials against COA (Certificate of Analysis) specs.
3. `qc_pass_fail_evaluator`: Checks physical and chemical parameters against tolerance limits.
4. `quarantine_lot_manager`: Isolates defective batches to prevent production release.
5. `defect_classification_engine`: Categorizes non-conformances into Critical, Major, and Minor.
6. `capa_workflow_trigger`: Initiates Corrective and Preventive Action tickets for recurring defects.
7. `vendor_quality_rating`: Penalizes supplier scorecards for rejected incoming lots.
8. `in_process_quality_audit`: Validates shop floor production order checkpoints during manufacturing.
9. `coa_document_parser`: Extracts test readings from vendor inspection certificates.
10. `rework_scrap_decision_rule`: Evaluates cost of rework vs scrap disposal.
